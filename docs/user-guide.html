1. What This Dashboard Does

The Lexie's LinkedIn Aggregator automatically finds and displays LinkedIn posts from people who are hiring, looking for freelancers, or discussing topics you care about — all in one place, updated twice a day.

Instead of manually scrolling through LinkedIn every day hoping to catch the right posts, this dashboard does the searching for you. You tell it what kinds of opportunities you're looking for (e.g., "Freelance Video Editor", "Hiring Producer"), and it automatically collects matching LinkedIn posts and shows them in a clean, easy-to-browse feed.

Automated Search

Searches LinkedIn for your chosen keywords so you don't have to scroll endlessly.

Organised Feed

All matching posts stored in one searchable feed, sorted by date.

Twice-Daily Updates

Automatically refreshes at two times you choose each day — no manual work needed.

One-Click Outreach

Copy any post with your custom message template attached — ready to paste into Claude or ChatGPT.

Smart Filtering

Filter by location, date range, or keywords to find the most relevant posts quickly.

Engagement Data

See likes, comments, and shares at a glance to spot the most active opportunities.

How it works in plain English Behind the scenes, the dashboard uses a service called Apify to scrape (collect) public LinkedIn posts matching your search terms. Those posts are saved to a secure private database (Supabase), and the dashboard displays them in real time. Your credentials are stored only in your browser — nothing is shared with third parties.

2. What You Need

Because the dashboard is already deployed online, you only need two things: a Supabase account (your private database) and an Apify account (the scraping service). Both are free to start. Total time: about 5 minutes.
Account What it's for Cost Where to sign up
Supabase Your private database that stores all the LinkedIn posts the dashboard collects Free tier supabase.com
Apify The service that automatically collects (scrapes) LinkedIn posts matching your search terms Free starter plan apify.com
Apify usage note Apify's free plan includes a limited monthly scraping budget. For regular use (twice-daily scrapes with up to 10 posts each), the free tier is typically sufficient. If you need more posts or higher frequency, a paid plan starts at around $49/month.

3. First-Time Setup

The dashboard is already live and running — you just need to connect it to your own Supabase database and Apify account by pasting your credentials into the Settings panel. This takes about 5 minutes and only needs to be done once per browser.
Your credentials are already saved If Bryan pasted your credentials on your behalf during handover, they are already in your browser. Simply open the dashboard, go to the Settings tab, and click Verify Schema Connection to confirm everything is working. If it shows a green tick, you're all set — skip to Section 4.

Step A — Get Your Supabase Credentials

Log in to Supabase

Go to supabase.com and sign in to your account. Open your LinkedIn Dashboard project.

Find your API credentials

In the left sidebar, click Project Settings → API. You'll see two values you need:

  • Project URL — looks like https://abcdefgh.supabase.co
  • anon public key — a long string starting with eyJ...

Copy each one and keep them somewhere handy (e.g., a note on your desktop) — you'll paste them into the dashboard next.

The anon public key is safe to use inside the dashboard Settings panel. Do not share it publicly (e.g., don't post it on social media or send it in a public chat). Treat it like a private password.

Step B — Get Your Apify API Token

Log in to Apify

Go to apify.com and sign in. Click your profile icon in the top-right corner, then go to Settings → API & Integrations.

Copy your Personal API Token

Under Personal API tokens, copy the token shown. It starts with apify_api_. Keep it somewhere handy alongside your Supabase credentials.

Step C — Enter Your Credentials in the Dashboard

Open the dashboard

Go to lexie-linkedin-aggregate.vercel.app in your browser.

Open the Settings tab

Click the Settings tab in the navigation bar at the top of the page.

Enter your Supabase credentials

In the Database section (the first section), paste your Project URL and Anon Public Key into the two fields provided. Then click Verify Schema Connection.

You should see a green success message confirming your database is connected correctly.

Enter your Apify token

Scroll down to the Scraper Settings section and paste your Apify API token into the Apify API Token field.

Add your search keywords

In the Search Queries section, type a keyword and press Enter to add it. Repeat for each search term you want the dashboard to monitor (e.g., Freelance Video Editor, Hiring Producer).

Save your settings

Scroll to the bottom and click Save All Settings. Your credentials are now saved in your browser — you won't need to enter them again unless you clear your browser cache or switch to a different browser.

Do your first manual refresh

Click the Refresh Feed button in the top navigation bar. Wait 1–3 minutes. Your first batch of LinkedIn posts will appear in the Feed tab automatically.

Switching browsers or devices? Your credentials are stored in the browser you used during setup. If you open the dashboard in a different browser (Chrome → Firefox, for example) or on a different device, just repeat Steps C3 and C4 to paste your credentials again — everything else (your posts, keywords, settings) is stored in the database and will be there waiting for you.

4. Configuring the Dashboard

Click the Settings tab in the top navigation bar to open the settings panel. All changes are saved to your database when you click Save All Settings at the bottom.

Section 1 — Database Connection

This is where you paste your Supabase Project URL and anon public key (covered in Section 3). Click Verify Schema Connection to confirm the connection is working — you should see a green success message. If credentials were already entered during handover, this should already show as connected.

Section 2 — Search Queries

Enter the keywords you want to search for on LinkedIn. Press Enter after each keyword to add it as a tag. Examples:

  • Freelance Video Editor
  • Hiring Producer
  • Looking for Social Media Manager
  • Content Creator Opportunity

Each keyword runs as a separate LinkedIn search — more keywords means more posts, but also uses more of your Apify budget.

Section 3 — Scraper Settings

SettingWhat it doesRecommended
Max Posts How many posts to collect per keyword per run 10–25
Time Filter Only collect posts from the last Day / Week / Month Week
Sort By Sort results by Date (newest first) or Relevance Date
Content Type Filter to posts with images, videos, or all content All
Scrape Pages How many pages of results to scan (1 page ≈ 10 posts) 1–2
Apify API Token Your secret Apify token from Phase B Required
Author Keywords Only collect posts from authors whose name contains these words Leave blank unless needed
Author URLs Collect posts only from specific LinkedIn profile URLs Leave blank unless needed

Section 4 — Extended Data Collection

These are optional toggles that collect more detail from each post, at the cost of slightly more Apify usage.

  • Scrape Reactions — Collects who reacted to the post (👍 ❤️ etc.) and how many. Leave off unless you need this detail.
  • Scrape Comments — Collects the comments on each post. Useful for understanding context, but increases scraping time.

Section 5 — Auto-Refresh Schedule

Pick two times per day when you'd like the dashboard to automatically scrape LinkedIn. The times are set in your local timezone and automatically converted to the correct format in the database. A good choice is once in the morning (e.g., 9:00 AM) and once in the early afternoon (e.g., 2:00 PM).

Section 6 — Claude Prompt

When you click the Copy button on any post, this text is prepended to the copied content. Use it to set up a reusable AI prompt for writing outreach messages. For example:

"Based on this LinkedIn post, write a short, personalised outreach message for a freelance video editor reaching out to this person. Keep it under 4 sentences, friendly and professional."

After copying, you paste the result into Claude (claude.ai) or ChatGPT to get a personalised message instantly.

Don't forget to save! After changing any settings, scroll to the bottom and click Save All Settings. Changes are not applied until saved.

5. Using the Dashboard Every Day

Once set up, your daily workflow is simple: open the dashboard, browse the feed, and copy posts you want to act on. The scraper runs automatically — you don't need to do anything to keep the feed fresh.

Searching & Filtering

Search bar

Type any word or phrase in the search bar at the top of the feed. The dashboard instantly filters to show only posts matching that text (in the post content or the poster's name).

Date filter pills

Click one of the date buttons to narrow the feed by recency:

  • Today — Posts scraped today
  • Week — Posts from the last 7 days
  • Month — Posts from the last 30 days
  • Year — Posts from the last 12 months
  • All — All posts ever collected

Filters panel

Click the Filters button to open advanced filters:

  • Location include tags — Only show posts that mention these locations (e.g., London, Remote)
  • Location exclude tags — Hide posts that mention these locations (e.g., USA, India)

These filters are saved to your database automatically and persist across sessions.

Refreshing the Feed Manually

Click the Refresh Feed button (top-right of the navigation bar) to trigger a scrape immediately — without waiting for the next scheduled time. This is useful when you've just added new keywords or want the very latest posts right now.

Why does refreshing take a moment? After clicking Refresh Feed, the dashboard sends a request to start scraping. The scraper runs in the background and can take 1–3 minutes depending on how many keywords you have. New posts will appear in the feed automatically as soon as they're collected — no need to reload the page.

Outreach Workflow

Find a promising post

Browse the feed or search for a keyword. Look for posts from people who are actively hiring or looking for exactly your skills.

Click the Copy button on the post card. The post content plus your Claude prompt is now in your clipboard.

Open claude.ai or chatgpt.com, paste (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V), and press Enter. The AI will write a personalised outreach message based on the post.

6. How Automatic Refreshes Work

Once set up, the dashboard refreshes itself automatically at the two times you've configured — no action required from you.

The refresh schedule works as follows:

  1. At the scheduled times, a database job (called a cron job) automatically triggers the scraper.
  2. The scraper reads your active search keywords from the database.
  3. For each keyword, it calls Apify to collect matching LinkedIn posts.
  4. New posts are saved to the database and appear in your feed in real time.
  5. Duplicate posts (posts you already have) are automatically skipped — you won't see the same post twice.
Changing your schedule You can change your refresh times at any time in the Settings → Auto-Refresh Schedule section. Enter the times in your local timezone — the dashboard converts them automatically. Remember to click Save All Settings after making changes.

7. Troubleshooting

Here are the most common issues and how to fix them quickly.

Dashboard shows a blank screen or won't load

  • Make sure you're visiting the correct URL: lexie-linkedin-aggregate.vercel.app
  • Try doing a hard refresh: press Ctrl + Shift + R (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + R (Mac).
  • Try opening the dashboard in a different browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) to rule out a browser issue.
  • If the page loads but shows no data, check that your credentials are entered correctly in the Settings tab.

Opened the dashboard in a new browser and nothing is saved

  • This is expected — credentials are saved per browser. Open the Settings tab, paste your Supabase URL, anon key, and Apify token, then click Save All Settings.
  • Your posts and keywords are stored in the database, so they will reappear once the credentials are re-entered.

"Connection failed" when testing the database

  • Double-check the Supabase URL and anon key in Settings — make sure there are no extra spaces at the beginning or end.
  • Make sure you ran all three CREATE TABLE SQL statements and didn't get an error.
  • Verify your Supabase project is not paused (free projects pause after a period of inactivity — log in to supabase.com and check).

Feed is empty after clicking "Refresh Feed"

  • Make sure you've added at least one search keyword in Settings → Search Queries.
  • Check that your Apify API token is correct — test it by logging into apify.com and checking your account.
  • Wait 2–3 minutes after clicking Refresh — scraping takes time.
  • Check the Date filter — if it's set to Today and no posts were scraped yet today, switch to All.

The automatic schedule isn't running

  • Verify the pg_cron SQL was run successfully in Supabase (no error message when you ran it).
  • Make sure the Edge Function is deployed — run supabase functions deploy linkedin-refresh --no-verify-jwt again if unsure.
  • Note that scheduled times are in UTC — if you're on a different timezone, double-check the conversion.

"Refresh Feed" shows an error banner

  • The automation script may not be deployed. Run the deploy command from Phase D, Step 4.
  • Check that the Supabase Edge Function is listed under Edge Functions in your Supabase project dashboard.

Posts not showing despite a successful refresh

  • LinkedIn may not have returned results for your keywords on that particular run — try broader keywords.
  • Apify's free plan may have run out of monthly compute budget — check your usage on apify.com.
  • Make sure the Time Filter in Settings isn't too narrow (e.g., set to "Day" when posts were published a week ago).

8. Glossary

Plain-English definitions for the technical terms used in this guide.
TermPlain-English Explanation
Supabase A cloud service that acts as your dashboard's private database. Think of it as a very organised spreadsheet stored securely online, which your dashboard reads and writes to.
Apify A service that visits websites (like LinkedIn) and collects data from them automatically. It's like sending a very fast robot to scroll through LinkedIn and copy down posts for you.
Scraping The process of automatically reading and collecting data from a website. The dashboard "scrapes" LinkedIn to get the posts.
API Token / API Key A secret password that identifies you when your dashboard communicates with a service like Apify or Supabase. Never share these with others.
Edge Function A small program that runs on Supabase's servers (not on your computer). It handles the actual scraping in the background.
Cron job A scheduled task that runs automatically at specific times — like setting an alarm that triggers the scraper without you having to do anything.
Realtime A feature that makes new posts appear in your feed instantly without you needing to reload the page.
RLS / Row-Level Security A database security setting. The SQL you ran in setup configures it to allow the dashboard to access your data.
UTC Universal Time Coordinated — a global time standard. Your schedule times are stored in UTC internally but displayed in your local timezone in the dashboard.
Vercel deployment The process of publishing the dashboard to the internet so it can be accessed at a public URL. This was done once during the build — you don't need to do anything to keep it running.
Anon Key Short for "anonymous key" — a Supabase key that allows the dashboard to connect to your database. It's safe to use in the dashboard settings but should not be published publicly.
SQL Structured Query Language — the commands used to set up the database tables. This was handled during the build; you don't need to use or understand SQL to operate the dashboard.
Browser local storage A small storage area inside your browser where the dashboard saves your credentials (Supabase URL, key, Apify token). It's specific to each browser and persists until you clear your cache. Your posts and settings are stored in the database, not in local storage.
Vercel The hosting service that serves the dashboard website. It keeps the dashboard available 24/7 at lexie-linkedin-aggregate.vercel.app without you needing to run anything on your computer.
