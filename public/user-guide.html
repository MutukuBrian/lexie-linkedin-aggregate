1. What This Dashboard Does

Lexie's LinkedIn Aggregator automatically finds and displays two types of LinkedIn content — job postings and people's posts sharing opportunities — all in one place, updated twice a day.

Instead of manually scrolling through LinkedIn every day, this dashboard does the searching for you. It collects both official LinkedIn job listings and posts people share on their feed (e.g. "We're hiring!" or "My company has an opening for…"). People's posts are often more authentic than formal job board listings, and they frequently mention roles that never appear on the jobs board at all.

Jobs Feed

Scrapes LinkedIn's official jobs board for your chosen titles and location.

Posts Feed

Scrapes LinkedIn posts — people sharing real opportunities to their feed. Often more authentic than job listings.

All-in-One Feed

Switch between All, Posts, and Jobs views in one click. Both sources merged and sorted newest-first.

Twice-Daily Updates

Automatically refreshes at two times you choose each day - no manual work needed.

One-Click Outreach

Copy any job or post with your custom prompt attached - ready to paste into Claude or ChatGPT for a tailored cover letter or outreach message.

Cost Controls

Toggle either scraper on or off from Settings to avoid unnecessary Apify charges when you only need one feed.

How it works in plain English Behind the scenes, the dashboard uses a service called Apify to scrape (collect) public LinkedIn content. The jobs scraper uses the worldunboxer/rapid-linkedin-scraper actor and the posts scraper uses the harvestapi/linkedin-post-search actor. All results are saved to your secure private Supabase database and appear in real time. Your credentials are stored only in your browser — nothing is shared with third parties.

2. What You Need

Because the dashboard is already deployed online, you only need two things: a Supabase account (your private database) and an Apify account (the scraping service). Both are free to start. Total time: about 5 minutes.
Account What it's for Cost Where to sign up
Supabase Your private database that stores all the LinkedIn jobs the dashboard collects Free tier supabase.com
Apify The service that automatically collects (scrapes) LinkedIn job listings matching your search terms Free starter plan apify.com
Apify usage note Apify's free plan includes a limited monthly scraping budget. For regular use (twice-daily scrapes with around 100 jobs per title), the free tier is typically sufficient. If you need higher volume or frequency, a paid plan starts at around $49/month.

3. First-Time Setup

The dashboard is already live and running - you just need to connect it to your own Supabase database and Apify account by pasting your credentials into the Settings panel. This takes about 5 minutes and only needs to be done once per browser.
Your credentials are already saved If Bryan pasted your credentials on your behalf during handover, they are already in your browser. Simply open the dashboard, go to the Settings tab, and click Verify Schema Connection to confirm everything is working. If it shows a green tick, you're all set - skip to Section 4.

Step A - Get Your Supabase Credentials

Log in to Supabase

Go to supabase.com and sign in to your account. Open your LinkedIn Dashboard project.

Find your API credentials

In the left sidebar, click Project Settings → API. You'll see two values you need:

  • Project URL - looks like https://abcdefgh.supabase.co
  • anon public key - a long string starting with eyJ...

Copy each one and keep them somewhere handy (e.g., a note on your desktop) - you'll paste them into the dashboard next.

The anon public key is safe to use inside the dashboard Settings panel. Do not share it publicly (e.g., don't post it on social media or send it in a public chat). Treat it like a private password.

Step B - Get Your Apify API Token

Log in to Apify

Go to apify.com and sign in. Click your profile icon in the top-right corner, then go to Settings → API & Integrations.

Copy your Personal API Token

Under Personal API tokens, copy the token shown. It starts with apify_api_. Keep it somewhere handy alongside your Supabase credentials.

Step C - Enter Your Credentials in the Dashboard

Open the dashboard

Go to lexie-linkedin-aggregate.vercel.app in your browser.

Open the Settings tab

Click the Settings tab in the navigation bar at the top of the page.

Enter your Supabase credentials

In the Database section (the first section), paste your Project URL and Anon Public Key into the two fields provided. Then click Verify Schema Connection.

You should see a green success message confirming your database is connected correctly.

Enter your Apify token(s)

Scroll down to Scraper Settings (Apify Engine) and paste your Apify API token into the Apify API Token field. Then do the same in the Posts Scraper Settings section — it can be the same token; both scrapers run under your Apify account.

Add your job titles and post search queries

In the Job title searches section, type a job title and press Enter to add it (e.g., Creative Project Manager, Digital Producer). Then set a Location in Scraper Settings.

In the Post searches section, add LinkedIn search queries to find people's posts about openings (e.g., Digital Producer NYC hiring, Video Editor remote opportunity). These are the same queries you'd type into LinkedIn's search bar.

Save your settings

When any setting changes, a Save All Settings button will appear at the bottom of the page — click it to persist everything. Your credentials are saved in your browser and all other config is stored in the database.

Do your first manual refresh

In the Feed tab, make sure the view is set to All, then click Refresh Feed in the top navigation bar. Wait 1–3 minutes. Jobs and posts will appear automatically as they're collected — no page reload needed.

Switching browsers or devices? Your credentials are stored in the browser you used during setup. If you open the dashboard in a different browser (Chrome → Firefox, for example) or on a different device, just repeat Steps C3 and C4 to paste your credentials again - everything else (your jobs, job titles, settings) is stored in the database and will be there waiting for you.

4. Configuring the Dashboard

Click the Settings tab in the top navigation bar to open the settings panel. All changes are saved to your database when you click Save All Settings at the bottom.

Section 1 - Database Connection

This is where you paste your Supabase Project URL and anon public key (covered in Section 3). Click Verify Schema Connection to confirm the connection is working - you should see a green success message. If credentials were already entered during handover, this should already show as connected.

Section 2 - Scraper Toggles

Two toggle buttons let you independently enable or disable each scraper:

  • Jobs scraper — controls the LinkedIn jobs board scraper. When off, the Refresh button skips it and no Apify credits are used for jobs.
  • Posts scraper — controls the LinkedIn posts feed scraper. When off, the Refresh button skips it.

Turn one off if you only want the other. For example, if you already have plenty of job listings and only want fresh posts today, toggle the jobs scraper off temporarily. Toggling is instant — just click the button and it auto-saves.

Save Apify credits Each scrape run costs Apify credits. If you only need one type of content on a given day, turn the other scraper off before clicking Refresh Feed.

Section 3 - Job Title Searches

Enter the job titles you want to monitor on LinkedIn Jobs. Press Enter after each title to add it as a tag. Examples:

  • Creative Project Manager
  • Digital Producer
  • Content Strategist
  • Social Media Manager

Each job title runs as a separate LinkedIn Jobs search, using the global Location and filters configured in Section 4. More titles means more jobs but uses more of your Apify budget.

Section 4 - Post Searches

Enter the search queries you want to use for the LinkedIn posts feed. These are the same phrases you'd type into LinkedIn's search bar when looking for posts about opportunities. Press Enter after each one. Examples:

  • Digital Producer NYC hiring
  • Creative Project Manager remote opportunity
  • Video Editor we're hiring
  • Content Strategist looking for

Each query runs a separate posts scrape. Results are people's posts — not job listings — so you'll often find opportunities that never made it to the official jobs board.

Section 7 - Auto-Refresh Schedule

Pick two times per day when you'd like the dashboard to automatically scrape LinkedIn. The times are set in your local timezone and automatically converted to the correct format in the database. A good choice is once in the morning (e.g., 9:00 AM) and once in the early afternoon (e.g., 2:00 PM).

Section 8 - Claude Prompt

When you click the Copy button on any job card, this text is prepended to the copied content (which includes the job title, company, location, employment type, salary if available, and the full description). Use it to set up a reusable AI prompt for writing cover letters. For example:

"Based on this LinkedIn job posting and my CV, write a tailored cover letter. Highlight the experience that most closely matches the role's requirements. Keep it under one page, professional and warm."

After copying, you paste the result into Claude (claude.ai) or ChatGPT to get a personalised cover letter instantly.

Don't forget to save! After changing any settings, scroll to the bottom and click Save All Settings. Changes are not applied until saved.

5. Using the Dashboard Every Day

Once set up, your daily workflow is simple: open the dashboard, browse the feed, and act on the opportunities that match. The scraper runs automatically - you don't need to do anything to keep the feed fresh.

Feed View Modes

Inside the Feed tab, three view modes let you focus on what matters today:

  • All — Jobs and posts merged together, sorted by newest first.
  • Posts — Only people's LinkedIn posts sharing opportunities.
  • Jobs — Only official LinkedIn job board listings.

Switch between them using the segmented control in the filter bar. Your selection is remembered between sessions.

Why posts are valuable A lot of NYC and remote job listings on LinkedIn's official board are fake or already filled. People who share real openings to their feed tend to be actively recruiting — these opportunities are often fresher and more legitimate.

Searching & Filtering

Search bar

Type any word or phrase to instantly filter the feed. For jobs, it searches title, company, and description. For posts, it searches the post text and author name.

Date filter pills

Click one of the date buttons to narrow the feed by recency:

  • Last 24h – Items from the past 24 hours
  • Week – Items from the last 7 days
  • Month – Items from the last 30 days
  • Year – Items from the last 12 months
  • All – Everything ever collected

Filters panel

Click the Filters button for advanced controls:

  • Easy Apply only (jobs view) — filter to one-click applications
  • Employment type (jobs view) — Full-time, Contract, Part-time, etc.
  • Seniority level (jobs view) — Entry level, Mid-Senior, Director, etc.
  • Show hidden — reveals all hidden items as faded cards so you can unhide them
  • Match in content / location — only show items containing this text
  • Hide items containing — permanently filter out items with these keywords (e.g. unpaid, commission only)

Note: Employment type, seniority, and Easy Apply filters only apply to job cards — they're hidden automatically in Posts view.

Refreshing the Feed Manually

Click the Refresh Feed button (top-right of the navigation bar) to trigger a scrape immediately. The button is smart about which view you're in:

  • All view — fires both the jobs scraper and the posts scraper in parallel
  • Jobs view — fires only the jobs scraper
  • Posts view — fires only the posts scraper

Each scraper respects its own toggle — if it's switched off in Settings, the edge function exits early without spending any Apify credits.

Why does refreshing take a moment? After clicking Refresh Feed, scraping runs in the background — it can take 1–3 minutes depending on how many keywords you have. New results appear automatically in the feed as soon as they land — no page reload needed.

6. How Automatic Refreshes Work

Once set up, the dashboard refreshes itself automatically at the two times you've configured - no action required from you.

The refresh schedule works as follows:

  1. At the scheduled times, a database cron job automatically triggers both edge functions (jobs and posts) — each respects its own is_active toggle, so a disabled scraper exits immediately without spending credits.
  2. The jobs scraper reads your active job titles and settings, then calls Apify for each title.
  3. The posts scraper reads your active post search queries and settings, then calls Apify for each query.
  4. New items are saved to the database and appear in your feed in real time.
  5. Duplicates (matched by LinkedIn post ID or job ID) are automatically updated, not duplicated — you won't see the same item twice.
Changing your schedule You can change your refresh times at any time in the Settings → Auto-Refresh Schedule section. Enter the times in your local timezone - the dashboard converts them automatically. Remember to click Save All Settings after making changes.

7. Troubleshooting

Here are the most common issues and how to fix them quickly.

Dashboard shows a blank screen or won't load

  • Make sure you're visiting the correct URL: lexie-linkedin-aggregate.vercel.app
  • Try doing a hard refresh: press Ctrl + Shift + R (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + R (Mac).
  • Try opening the dashboard in a different browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) to rule out a browser issue.
  • If the page loads but shows no data, check that your credentials are entered correctly in the Settings tab.

Opened the dashboard in a new browser and nothing is saved

  • This is expected - credentials are saved per browser. Open the Settings tab, paste your Supabase URL, anon key, and Apify token, then click Save All Settings.
  • Your jobs and job titles are stored in the database, so they will reappear once the credentials are re-entered.

"Connection failed" when testing the database

  • Double-check the Supabase URL and anon key in Settings - make sure there are no extra spaces at the beginning or end.
  • Make sure all three tables were created and the RLS policies applied correctly.
  • Verify your Supabase project is not paused (free projects pause after a period of inactivity - log in to supabase.com and check).

Moving to a new Supabase account or project

  • A full migration export (migration-export.sql) is included in the project files. Paste it into the new project's SQL Editor to recreate all three tables, enable Row Level Security, apply every policy, and seed the scraper settings row in one step.
  • After running the SQL, update your Supabase URL and anon key in the dashboard Settings panel and click Verify Schema Connection.
  • Your applied/hidden job states and search keywords are preserved in the export. Job listings will refill automatically on the next scheduled scrape.

Jobs feed is empty after clicking "Refresh Feed"

  • Make sure the Jobs scraper toggle (Settings → Scraper Toggles) is switched on.
  • Make sure you've added at least one job title in Settings → Job title searches.
  • Make sure a Location is set in Scraper Settings (without one, results may be empty).
  • Check that your Apify API token (jobs) is correct — test it by logging into apify.com.
  • Wait 2–3 minutes after clicking Refresh — scraping takes time.
  • Check the Date filter — if it's set to Last 24h and no jobs were scraped today yet, switch to All.

Posts feed is empty after clicking "Refresh Feed"

  • Make sure the Posts scraper toggle (Settings → Scraper Toggles) is switched on.
  • Make sure you've added at least one query in Settings → Post searches.
  • Make sure the Apify API token (posts) field in Posts Scraper Settings is filled in — it can be the same token as the jobs scraper.
  • Try broadening your post search queries — very specific phrases may return 0 results.
  • The Posted limit defaults to 24h — if no posts matched in that window, switch it to week in Posts Scraper Settings and refresh again.
  • Make sure you're on the Posts or All view tab in the Feed — the Jobs view won't show posts.

The automatic schedule isn't running

  • Verify the pg_cron SQL was run successfully in Supabase (no error message when you ran it).
  • Make sure both Edge Functions are deployed — linkedin-refresh and linkedin-posts-refresh should both appear under Edge Functions in the Supabase dashboard.
  • Note that scheduled times are in UTC — if you're on a different timezone, double-check the conversion.

"Refresh Feed" shows an error banner

  • Check that both Edge Functions (linkedin-refresh and linkedin-posts-refresh) are deployed and show status Active in Supabase → Edge Functions.
  • A "partial failure" banner means one scraper succeeded and the other failed — check which one and investigate that function's logs in Supabase.

Jobs not showing despite a successful refresh

  • LinkedIn may not have returned results for your job titles in that location — try broader titles or a wider location.
  • Apify's free plan may have run out of monthly compute budget — check your usage on apify.com.
  • Make sure the Job posting time filter isn't too narrow (e.g. "Past 24 hours" when no fresh jobs were posted today).
  • Check whether your Experience level / Job type / Work schedule filters are too restrictive — set them to "Any" to widen the search.

A post keyword I added isn't showing in the database

  • Make sure you clicked Save All Settings after adding the keyword. The button only appears when there are unsaved changes.
  • Check the browser console (F12 → Console) for any error starting with [Post keywords sync].
  • Verify that the posts_search_keywords_lexiecoon table exists in your Supabase project — it's created by the posts migration SQL.
Still stuck? Send a screenshot of the error and the page you're on to Brian Mutuku (brianmutuku.dev@gmail.com) for support.

8. Glossary

Plain-English definitions for the technical terms used in this guide.
TermPlain-English Explanation
Supabase A cloud service that acts as your dashboard's private database. Think of it as a very organised spreadsheet stored securely online, which your dashboard reads and writes to.
Apify A service that visits websites (like LinkedIn) and collects data from them automatically. It's like sending a very fast robot to scroll through LinkedIn Jobs and copy down listings for you.
Scraping The process of automatically reading and collecting data from a website. The dashboard "scrapes" LinkedIn Jobs to get the listings.
API Token / API Key A secret password that identifies you when your dashboard communicates with a service like Apify or Supabase. Never share these with others.
Edge Function A small program that runs on Supabase's servers (not on your computer). It handles the actual scraping in the background.
Cron job A scheduled task that runs automatically at specific times - like setting an alarm that triggers the scraper without you having to do anything.
Realtime A feature that makes new jobs appear in your feed instantly without you needing to reload the page.
RLS / Row-Level Security A database security setting. The SQL you ran in setup configures it to allow the dashboard to access your data.
UTC Universal Time Coordinated - a global time standard. Your schedule times are stored in UTC internally but displayed in your local timezone in the dashboard.
Vercel deployment The process of publishing the dashboard to the internet so it can be accessed at a public URL. This was done once during the build - you don't need to do anything to keep it running.
Anon Key Short for "anonymous key" - a Supabase key that allows the dashboard to connect to your database. It's safe to use in the dashboard settings but should not be published publicly.
SQL Structured Query Language - the commands used to set up the database tables. This was handled during the build; you don't need to use or understand SQL to operate the dashboard.
Browser local storage A small storage area inside your browser where the dashboard saves your credentials (Supabase URL, key, Apify token). It's specific to each browser and persists until you clear your cache. Your jobs and settings are stored in the database, not in local storage.
Vercel The hosting service that serves the dashboard website. It keeps the dashboard available 24/7 at lexie-linkedin-aggregate.vercel.app without you needing to run anything on your computer.
